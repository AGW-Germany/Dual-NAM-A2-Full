Gateway Stereo Plus changelog
https://github.com/Naaxas/GatewayStereoPlus

11/08/2026 - v1.0.0  First public release. Based on Neural Amp Modeler 0.7.15.

  Added:   dual-mono processing (independent model, EQ, IR and DC blocker per
           channel); stereo-linked noise gate; inverse gain Link for
           level-matched A/B; delay-compensated dry path with Mix, Delta,
           Matched Bypass and a DIFF readout; two meter styles.
  Changed: output mode now defaults to Raw, not Normalized; gain changes glide
           across one block instead of stepping at the block boundary.
  Fixed:   17 defects, including three inherited from upstream that crashed the
           host. See CHANGELOG.md and AUDIT_REPORT.md.

Version numbering restarts at 1.0.0. It is not a continuation of upstream's.
